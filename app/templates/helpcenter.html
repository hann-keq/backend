Help Center

Find answers to frequently asked questions about managing your pets

How do I add a new pet?

You can add a new pet by clicking the "Add Pet" button in your profile under "My Pets" section.

How do I update my profile?

Go to your profile page and click "Edit Profile" to quickly update your personal information and profile picture.

How do I manage my bookings?

You can track, reschedule, or manage your grooming appointments under the "Booking" section from the navigation.

How do I contact support?

You can contact our live support team through the WhatsApp channel or use the active chat option available on your dashboard.

Still need help?

Our customer support is available to assist you with any advanced technical or ordering issues.
